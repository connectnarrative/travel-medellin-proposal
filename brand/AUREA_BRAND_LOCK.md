# AUREA — Approved Creative Identity V1.0
Approved creative direction: 2026-10-14. This is a design approval, NOT trademark clearance.

- **Master name:** AUREA (without accent, uppercase)
- **Descriptor:** PRIVATE ACCESS
- **Tagline:** PRIVATE ACCESS TO THE EXTRAORDINARY
- **Destination signature:** MEDELLÍN · COLOMBIA · BEYOND
- **Business:** curated luxury marketplace + private concierge; Medellín first, with destination-flexible brand identity.
- **Emblem:** arched doorway and abstract key, as the visual metaphor for private access. Do not replace with generic clip-art keys.
- **Typography:** refined upright serif for wordmark/large editorial headlines (Playfair Display direction); clean sans for navigation, supporting copy and forms (Montserrat direction). Never italicize the wordmark.
- **Colors:** Obsidian `#171715`, Ivory Mist `#F4F0E8`, Champagne Gold `#BDA16E`, Stone `#93877D`, Andes Sage `#414638`.
- **Voice:** discreet, welcoming, assured, editorial. No invented superlatives, exclusivity, supplier relationships, prices, or guaranteed bookings.
- **Images:** premium architecture, sophisticated stays, dining, landscapes, nightlife, wellness and real service encounters. Demo images and unverified listings are clearly labeled and noindex.
- **Contact names/numbers appearing on AI brand mockups are fictional and must not be published.**
- **Public launch hold:** confirm Colombian SIC and international trademark conflicts, domains/social names, business registration and tourism obligations first.

This repository contains scalable SVG interpretations of the approved raster concept. Review kerning and print reproduction before use as final production artwork. The original approved presentation boards and a downloadable v1 identity kit are part of the project conversation artifacts.

## Scope
The approved brand identity supersedes the old Travel & Life Medellín *public-facing design*, not the underlying content management, protected partner data, inquiry flow, or operating contracts. Preserve the existing database, authorization, CMS and booking workflow when rebranding.
