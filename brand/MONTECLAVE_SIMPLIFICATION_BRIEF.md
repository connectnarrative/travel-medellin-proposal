# Monteclave — presentation cleanup

## Reusable design prompt

Refine the existing Monteclave website into a calm, image-led hospitality presentation. Preserve the approved mountain-and-key emblem, obsidian #141917, antique gold #C6A56C, warm ivory #EDE7DB, mountain green #34483D, and the “Medellín, unlocked.” signature. Use upright Cinzel for the signature, restrained serif headings and readable sans-serif UI.

Give each page a single purpose. The homepage should introduce the brand, offer six visual category entry points, show three sample experiences, and close with a trip-planning action. Keep the complete catalogue accessible through View all. Remove splash screens, repeated brand statements, numbered decorations, duplicate itinerary explanations and partner pricing from the traveler homepage.

Keep navigation compact: collection, partners, planning, saved items and language. Put secondary pages in the menu or footer. Avoid floating elements that cover content. Keep each service’s distinct existing photo and its truthful illustrative label.

For partner meetings, show one personalized business preview, two clearly priced packages, an expandable detailed comparison, the onboarding process, terms and one application form. Do not repeat personalization controls or introductory arguments. Preserve package prices and commercial terms. Make optional trip questions expandable. Use concise action labels, consistent spacing, restrained headings and readable mobile layouts. Never invent customers, available inventory, results or working backend capabilities. Preserve bilingual content, saved itineraries, redirects and accessibility.

Verify compilation, routes, asset references, package prices, links and endpoint failure behavior. Check browser layout and interactions at mobile, tablet and desktop widths when a permitted browser environment is available. Report unverified checks accurately.

## Audit findings and changes

- Homepage: ten competing sections reduced to four. Removed two audience entrance panels, repeated editorial explanation, the long eleven-category index, duplicate destination sections, a second itinerary invitation and commercial pricing.
- Header/footer: reduced navigation duplication, shortened saved-item label, removed fixed mobile saved bar; saved items remain in the mobile menu.
- Browsing: six photo-led entry points, full catalogue link, three sample profiles. All existing detail pages and distinct listing photos retained.
- Partner landing: removed repeated benefit explanation; retained sector previews, prices, disclosures and application link.
- Partner proposal: removed duplicate opening/personalization sections; kept a single interactive preview; detailed feature table is expandable.
- Forms: optional trip questions expandable; brief preparation and sharing remain explicit, with no false submission claim.
- Typography/layout: smaller headings, consistent section rhythm, quieter borders, responsive category grid and wrapping filter controls.
- Removed implementation-roadmap copy from the itinerary page.
